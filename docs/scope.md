## Problem statement
This project will build a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system for answering grounded questions about AI governance and open-model compliance documents. The system should retrieve relevant evidence from official sources and generate answers with citations.

## Included sources
- Official regulations and policy documents
- Official AI risk-management frameworks
- Official model cards from model providers
- Official license or terms-of-use documents for open-weight/open-source models
- Official technical guidance directly related to AI governance or model risk

## Excluded sources
- Blog posts
- Reddit discussions
- YouTube summaries
- News articles summarizing regulations
- SEO websites
- Opinion pieces without primary-source value
- Duplicate mirrors of official documents

## Corpus quality rules
- Prefer official publisher sources
- Preserve document version/date where possible
- Keep source URLs
- Avoid duplicate documents
- Track document type and jurisdiction
